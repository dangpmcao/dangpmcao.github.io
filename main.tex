\section{Research Interests}
My research interests lie in 3D/4D computer vision and medical image analysis, particularly spatiotemporal reconstruction, geometry-aware representation learning, and healthcare digital twins. I am interested in learning anatomical structure and dynamics from sparse or multimodal observations using mesh-based and implicit representations, with broader interests in robust and generalizable visual learning.
