ABOUT ME

Cao Pham Minh Dang

I am a Data Science undergraduate at VinUniversity, based in Hanoi, Vietnam. I work on problems in medical image analysis and bioinformatics, with the aim of building reliable computational tools that can better represent human anatomy and support clinical care.

My research interests lie in 3D/4D computer vision and medical image analysis, particularly spatiotemporal reconstruction, geometry-aware representation learning, and healthcare digital twins. I am interested in learning anatomical structure and dynamics from sparse or multimodal observations using mesh-based and implicit representations, with broader interests in robust and generalizable visual learning.

At VinUniversity, I conduct research under Dr. Huy Hieu Pham at the Computer Vision and Medical AI Lab. During my exchange at HKUST, I work as a research intern under Dr. Lequan Yu at the Medical AI Lab, The University of Hong Kong.
